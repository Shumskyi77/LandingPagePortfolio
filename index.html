The workshop

We don’t do much.
We do it properly.

A

Only solid wood

Oak, ash, walnut from two sawmills we’ve used for years. If a board is bad, it goes to firewood — not into your table.

B

One roof, four people

Sawing, joinery and oiling all happen at Drewniana 14. The van you see at delivery is ours too. Dog’s name is Puck.

C

5 years on the frame

A joint cracked or a door dropped? We come back and fix it. We still service a table from 2014 — leg re-glued, free.

WHAT WE WON’T DO

  • No chipboard or MDF “with wood effect”.
  • No gloss high-gloss plastic film. Oil or hardwax, that’s it.
  • No “ready in 3 days”. Wood doesn’t hurry.
